Find your flower
Every bloom has a story to tell. Explore the language of flowers and uncover the hidden meanings behind each petal.

Floriagraphy, also known as the language of flowers, was popularized in the Victorian era as a way to convey messages and emotions through the selection and arrangement of flowers. Each flower carries its own unique symbolism, allowing individuals to express feelings that may be difficult to articulate with words alone.

The song story

"La Vie en Rose" (meaning "Life in Pink" or seeing life through rose-colored glasses) was written in 1945, made famous by French icon Édith Piaf, and released as a single in 1947. Louis Armstrong recorded his famous English-language cover of "La Vie en Rose" on June 26, 1950, for Decca Records in New York City, with an arrangement by Sy Oliver
