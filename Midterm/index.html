Floriagraphy, also known as the language of flowers, was popularized in the Victorian era as a way to convey messages and emotions through the selection and arrangement of flowers. Each flower carries its own unique symbolism, allowing individuals to express feelings that may be difficult to articulate with words alone.

The song story

The song "calla" by the South Korean indie-pop band wave to earth (written by Daniel Kim) uses the calla lily as a central metaphor for resilience, tender love, and renewal in the face of hardship
The name "calla" addresses the flower (and symbolically a loved one). Even when "someone said that your time will be over soon," the lyrics reassure her that she "can always grow back". It is a message of healing and inner strength after setbacks or emotional pain.
