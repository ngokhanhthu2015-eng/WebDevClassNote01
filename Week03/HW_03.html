Vietnamese Spring Rolls

Gỏi cuốn, nem cuốn, salad roll, summer roll, fresh spring roll, or rice paper roll is a Vietnamese dish traditionally consisting of pork, prawn, vegetables, bún, and other ingredients wrapped in bánh tráng. Gỏi cuốn are served fresh, unlike similar rolls that are fried, like the Vietnamese chả giò.

Ingredients

  • Rice paper
  • Shrimp
  • Lettuce
  • Carrots

Instruction

  1. Prepare all the ingredients.
  2. Soak the rice paper.
  3. Place the ingredients on the rice paper.
  4. Roll the spring roll.
